THE QUEEN. A regal technological queen bee seen from the front, three times the bulk of a worker, seated upright on a faceted crystalline throne that is itself a large hexagonal cell. Her abdomen is long and armoured in overlapping cut-stone plates. Her thorax carries a large hexagonal core with a smaller inverted triangle engraved inside it. A crown of six thin crystalline spires rises from her head. Four folded engraved wings with fine vein lines. Calm, monumental, symmetrical.
